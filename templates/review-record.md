# Review record template

Copy for one review. Describe actual evidence separately from interpretation and a proposed decision.

- Review ID, revision, and time with timezone:
- Initiative, outcome, mandate, and delegation revisions reviewed:
- Review owner and participants:
- Trigger and previous decision reference:
- Original criteria, assumptions, and observation window:
- Metric observations with units, population, denominator, method, and source:
- Verified operational effects and their precise claim scopes:
- Unknown, partial, stale, missing, restricted, or contradictory evidence:
- Product outcome assessment and attribution limits:
- Resource allocation, commitments, observed spend, forecasts, and human effort:
- Risks, incidents, exceptions, and dependencies requiring attention:
- Affected people's feedback and recorded dissent:
- Options considered: continue, change, expand, pause, or retire:
- Selected action, rationale, and authorized decision reference:
- Resulting mandate or delegation revisions, if any:
- Requested operational controls and separately observed results:
- Follow-up owners, due conditions, and acceptance evidence:
- Learning candidates and reviewed learning records:
- Next review date or event trigger:
- Remaining obligations if paused or retired:
- Access and retention rules:
