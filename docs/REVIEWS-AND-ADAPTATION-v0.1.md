# Reviews and adaptation v0.1

Status: draft guidance. [Documentation index](README.md) · [Review template](../templates/review-record.md)

## Review triggers

Use scheduled reviews for recurring investment and mandate decisions, and event triggers for material changes. Examples include exhausted budget headroom, a changed provider guarantee, an unresolved consequential operation, a quality breach, a revoked permission, or evidence that contradicts a central assumption.

Each trigger identifies its owner, response expectation, available containment, and escalation route. Group repeated notifications about the same unresolved condition. Communicate when its severity, evidence, or required action changes.

## Review inputs and outputs

Compare the current outcome, mandate, and resources with the criteria established at the last decision. Include observations, contradictory evidence, unknown effects, human workload, dependencies, and total cost. Identify missing information and explain how it constrains the decision.

Record one or more explicit actions: continue within current authority, change the initiative, expand through a new decision, pause relevant work, or retire. Assign follow-up owners and a next review condition. A completed review is a management fact, not proof that every requested runtime change succeeded.

## Intervention and incidents

An urgent incident may require an authorized owner to request containment immediately. Specify whether the request prevents new dispatch, stops work at checkpoints, requests provider cancellation, or performs another bounded action. Record acknowledgement separately from observed effectiveness and disclose work already accepted by a provider.

Investigate unresolved effects through engineering evidence and reconciliation. Any effectful recovery returns through current authority. Keep the incident's operational record linked to the management review; preserve prior decisions and their original evidence.

## Learning and policy changes

Record which assumption was tested, what the evidence supports or contradicts, and where it remains inconclusive. Identify how the result affects a future decision. An interesting observation can remain a candidate learning until reviewed.

Changing a policy, budget, mandate, or delegation requires its normal authority and review path. An agent's retrospective cannot silently rewrite those controls. Add concrete failure scenarios to future evaluations where they can test the observed weakness.

## Retirement

Confirm which future actions have been disabled and which operations remain in flight or unresolved. Assign responsibility for those effects, ongoing customer obligations, credentials, retained evidence, and any required recovery. The initiative may stop receiving investment while those responsibilities continue.
