# Scope and non-claims

Updated: 2 October 2026. [Documentation index](README.md) · [Status](STATUS-AND-ROADMAP.md)

## Available artifacts

This repository contains a proposed management discipline, manifesto, twelve draft requirements, record and lifecycle guidance, operating guidance, companion integration mappings, templates, two fictional scenarios, a threat model, and planned assessment procedures. It also supplies an experimental JSON Schema profile, a Python/SQLite pilot, synthetic fixtures, focused tests, and a named local validation report.

The initial focus is managing one AI initiative and its human–AI work through an evidence-based review. Portfolio coordination and AI-assisted management are described as broader applications of the model.

## Not yet supplied

There is no production runtime, stable SDK, dashboard, management agent, provider adapter, or validated organizational adoption. No software interoperability with the Design or Engineering projects has been demonstrated here. General field names and lifecycle values remain proposals beyond the narrower experimental profile.

The pilot's identities are selected by its caller. Its role and scope checks demonstrate local application behavior, not real authentication or resistance to a database administrator. Evidence is synthetic or caller-supplied; provenance is recorded but not independently authenticated. A threshold assessment is not causal proof or complete product validation. Reservations are local accounting commitments, not actual spend or executable authority. The pilot cannot stop an external provider or verify an external effect.

## Limits

The framework does not replace accountable leadership, domain judgment, existing project or financial systems, or application enforcement. It cannot establish customer benefit from a successful tool call, causality from correlation, complete reversibility of external effects, or universal organizational suitability.

The requirements are this project's proposals. They are not legal advice, external certification, or evidence of compliance with another standard. Domain-specific obligations remain the adopting organization's responsibility.

Examples use fictional actors, dates, thresholds, and observations. They explain reasoning and record relationships; they are not recommended universal targets or reported experiment results.

## Versions and evidence

Document version v0.1 is not a package release, deployment version, or passed assessment. Cite a source commit when reviewing this evolving draft. Companion source revisions are pinned in the integration guides. New work in either companion does not automatically validate this repository.

Documentation consistency checks can establish that files and references are coherent. They cannot establish that a management process works in practice.
