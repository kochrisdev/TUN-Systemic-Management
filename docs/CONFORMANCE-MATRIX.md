# Proposed conformance matrix

[Documentation index](README.md) · [Specification](SPECIFICATION-v0.1.md) · [Pilot plan](PILOT.md)

All requirements are **unassessed**. The procedures below are plans, not completed checks. An assessment must name its product or organizational workflow, environment, specification source revision, reviewer, actual evidence, and limitations.

| Requirement | Proposed assessment procedure | Evidence to retain | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| TSM-001 | Inspect a charter with a known baseline and one with a missing baseline | Outcome, owner, criteria, explicit unknowns and measurement plan | Unassessed |
| TSM-002 | Compare an AI recommendation, an authorized decision, and a recurring policy decision | Subject revision, owner or policy provenance, alternatives, rationale | Unassessed |
| TSM-003 | Inspect parent/child delegation and attempt a scope or budget expansion | Delegation chain, denied expansion, shared resource accounting | Unassessed |
| TSM-004 | Edit material mandate content and revoke authority before dispatch | New revision, stale decision rejection, denied dispatch | Unassessed |
| TSM-005 | Race shared budget commitments and delay cost observations | Reservation or equivalent enforcement results and limit decision | Unassessed |
| TSM-006 | Supply stale, missing, contradictory, and verified-but-narrow evidence | Review showing claim scope and preserved limitations | Unassessed |
| TSM-007 | Trigger both scheduled and event-based reviews | Decisions, follow-up owners, next triggers, revised mandates | Unassessed |
| TSM-008 | Request a pause before dispatch and during an unknown provider effect | Control request, acknowledgement, effectiveness evidence, residual effects | Unassessed |
| TSM-009 | Expire an exception and attempt an unauthorized renewal | Denial, authority checks, expiry, authorized renewal history | Unassessed |
| TSM-010 | Inspect access-filtered views, correction history, and deleted evidence | Access results, linked revisions, updated evidence limitations | Unassessed |
| TSM-011 | Propose a learning-driven policy change and retire an initiative with unresolved work | Separate policy decision, revoked authority, residual obligation owner | Unassessed |
| TSM-012 | Review an assessment report against actual artifacts and runs | Scope, revision, per-requirement results, exceptions, limitations | Unassessed |

## Assessment language

Draft-specified means a requirement is written. Implemented means relevant software or operating practice exists. Validated means a named check produced a result within a stated scope. Published means an artifact is available through a stated channel. These are separate claims.

Use pass, fail, unassessed, or not applicable with reasons and evidence for a particular assessment. A subset of passed checks cannot establish complete conformance. There is no independent TUN certification program provided by this repository.
