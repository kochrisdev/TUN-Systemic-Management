# Project governance

TUN Systemic Management is maintained by Christopher Tun (`@kochrisdev`). It begins as a founder-led project with a draft management model and is open to feedback and contributions.

## Decisions and contributions

The maintainer accepts changes to the repository and its proposed specification. Contributors should explain the problem, proposed behavior, evidence, and impact on existing guidance. Material changes to record meaning or authority boundaries should include a concrete scenario and updates to affected documents.

The manifesto expresses commitments. The versioned specification defines proposed requirements. Examples and templates explain those requirements but do not override them. Conflicts should be resolved explicitly in the specification and corresponding guidance.

## Claims and evidence

The project distinguishes proposals, implementation, validation, and publication. Publishing a draft does not certify an adopting organization or implementation. There is currently no independent TUN certification program.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Their submitter remains responsible for reviewing the content, checking references, and accurately describing what was validated. This initial documentation was developed with AI assistance.
